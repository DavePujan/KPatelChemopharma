Guiding Philosophy

Caring for Society: The Ecosystem of Our Business

The founding elders at the K. Patel Group established our enterprise with a fundamental tenet: industrialization must generate sustainable societal value. As India transitioned from an agrarian economy to a global manufacturing powerhouse, our commitment to ‘Made in India’ was matched by our dedication to building local capabilities, nurturing rural livelihoods, and protecting community well-being.

Pillar 01 — Healthcare Access

Medical Aid & Hospital Infrastructure

Ensuring underprivileged and rural communities in surrounding villages receive timely, high-grade medical diagnostics, emergency care, and maternal health support.

  • ✓ Smt. Jayaben Mody Multispeciality Hospital: Ongoing donor partnership providing vital diagnostic testing equipment, critical supplies, and subsidized treatment in Ankleshwar.
  • ✓ Sanskar Deep Garbh Sankar Kendra: Dedicated support for maternal healthcare programs, prenatal wellness, and women's health clinics.
  • ✓ Direct supply of essential medicines, medical apparatus, and rural health screening initiatives in neighboring GIDC villages.
Pillar 02 — Education & Youth

Rural School Upgrades & Learning Tools

Fostering inclusive education by upgrading rural school infrastructure, supplying educational essentials, and removing economic barriers for underprivileged students.

  • ✓ School Infrastructure Renovation: Classroom flooring repair, structural renovation, and sanitation upgrades for local village schools promoted by trusts/government.
  • ✓ Study Material & Notebook Distribution: Annual drives distributing thousands of notebooks, textbooks, study kits, and school uniforms to rural students.
  • ✓ Classroom Furniture & Digital Tools: Providing study benches, desks, computer printers, and learning hardware to rural educational facilities.
Pillar 03 — Cleanliness & Hygiene

Swachh Bharat & Village Sanitation

Promoting dignified hygiene and rural public health through targeted sanitation infrastructure, community awareness, and clean water solutions.

  • ✓ Community Toilet Construction: Building modern, hygienic toilet blocks in surrounding rural villages and educational institutions to eliminate open defecation.
  • ✓ Sanitation Infrastructure: Installing reliable drainage, waste disposal facilities, and promoting community hygiene campaigns in Ankleshwar's rural belt.
  • ✓ Clean drinking water distribution and hygiene support for public utility areas.
Pillar 04 — Community Welfare

Direct Aid & Environmental Harmony

Providing compassionate community relief during seasonal hardships while upholding zero-compromise environmental stewardship across all operations.

  • ✓ Food Grain Distribution: Direct food security ration drives and essential provisions distributed to daily wage earners and vulnerable village families.
  • ✓ Seasonal Relief Supplies: Distributing rain umbrellas, protective equipment, and essential community utilities during monsoon seasons.
  • ✓ Ecological Synergy: In-house 95%+ RO water recovery, advanced ETP, solar energy park, and ISO 14001:2015 eco-compliance safeguarding regional water tables.
Major Healthcare Milestone

Smt. Jayaben Mody Multispeciality Hospital Support

Managed by the Ankleshwar Industrial Development Society (AIDS), Smt. Jayaben Mody Multispeciality Hospital stands as a premier non-profit medical lifeline in Bharuch district.

As a longstanding corporate donor, K. Patel Chemopharma supports this healthcare institution with state-of-the-art diagnostic testing equipment, specialized medical resources, and subsidization of treatments for local workers, rural families, and underprivileged patients.

Governance & Accountability

CSR Policy Framework & Legal Compliance

Our CSR activities operate under strict governance mandated by Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules. All programs are reviewed, approved, and monitored by our Board-appointed CSR Committee.

CSR Committee & Funding Allocation

The CSR Committee of K. Patel Chemopharma Private Limited is constituted in accordance with applicable laws, operating under direct oversight of the Board of Directors:

  • • Mandatory Spend: The Committee endeavors to spend at least 2% of the average net profit generated during the preceding 3 financial years on approved CSR initiatives.
  • • Project Evaluation: Rigorous pre-appraisal of community programs based on long-term societal impact and measurable outcomes.
  • • Regular Board Reporting: Periodical monitoring reports submitted to the Board of Directors outlining fund utilization and milestone progress.
  • • Statutory Disclosures: Comprehensive CSR disclosures published in the Annual Director's Report and Annual Return (Form MGT-7).
Governance Leadership: By order of the Board, chaired by Mr. Jitendra Patel, Chairman (DIN: 00028001).

Statutory Exclusions from CSR Scope

To maintain the highest level of ethical integrity and genuine public benefit, the following activities are strictly excluded from our CSR scope:

Normal Business Pursuits: Activities undertaken in the ordinary course of company business.
Employee-Exclusive Benefits: Projects or programs that benefit solely the employees of the company and their family members.
Political Contributions: Any direct or indirect donation/funding directed towards political parties or political causes.
Activities Outside India: Programs or initiatives undertaken outside Indian sovereign jurisdiction.
