Governance & Oversight

CSR Framework & Compliance

All CSR initiatives are governed by our Board-appointed CSR Committee in strict accordance with Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Board Oversight & Funding

  • Statutory Spend: Minimum 2% of average net profits (preceding 3 financial years) dedicated to approved CSR programs.
  • Project Appraisal: All initiatives undergo pre-evaluation for measurable, long-term community benefits.
  • Annual Disclosures: Complete CSR expenditure and progress reported annually in the Board's Report.

Guiding Principles

  • Community-Centric: Funds exclusively support public welfare, fully separate from commercial operations.
  • Local Impact: Primary focus on rural communities and families around our Ankleshwar manufacturing hub.
  • Strict Non-Affiliation: Zero political donations; support directed only to vetted non-profit trusts and institutions.
