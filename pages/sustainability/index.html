The 4R Environmental Doctrine

Guiding daily plant operations, synthesis protocols, and resource management at our Ankleshwar complex.

1. Refuse

Eliminate Hazards

Proactively refusing toxic synthesis intermediates and hazardous solvent precursors. We prioritize inherently safer, REACH-compliant green chemical pathways.

2. Reduce

Slash Consumption

Minimizing freshwater intake and reducing batch carbon intensity through high-efficiency reaction vessels, thermal insulation, and process automation.

3. Reuse

Closed-Loop Streams

Recovering steam condensate and recycling process wash waters internally, preventing unnecessary discharge and conserving thermal energy.

4. Recycle

RO Potable Recovery

Treating plant effluents through multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) to convert wastewater into potable-grade process water returned directly to synthesis lines.

Water Stewardship

In-House Effluent Treatment & Multi-Stage RO Plant

Our Ankleshwar manufacturing facility operates a dedicated, full-scale Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) coupled with high-recovery Reverse Osmosis (RO) technology. Industrial effluents undergo primary physicochemical treatment, biological degradation, and high-pressure membrane filtration to extract clean water suitable for reuse.
