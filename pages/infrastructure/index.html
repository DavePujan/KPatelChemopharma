Quality Control & Assurance

Comprehensive Quality Assurance & Analytical Testing

We aim to meet the maximum requirements of our valued global customers with full conformance to customer-defined quality parameters. This rigorous quality culture helped our group expand into international export markets since the 1980s. Today, over 80% of our production is exported primarily to America, Europe, Japan, Far East Asia, Australia, and worldwide.

Our in-house analytical laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art instrumentation supporting manufacturing operations with standardized testing for raw materials, synthesis intermediates, and finished dyestuff and pigment dispersions.

Analytical Capabilities

Key Laboratory & Testing Instrumentation

Our quality assurance team operates specialized instruments across chromatography, spectrometry, colorimetry, and physical rheology to guarantee batch uniformity and strict regulatory compliance.

HPLC: High Performance Liquid Chromatography for dye purity
Gas Chromatograph (G.C.): Volatile & solvent analysis
AAS: Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer for heavy metals
UV/VIS Spectrophotometer: Optical density & absorbance
Computerized Color Matching: Precise Delta E colorimetry
Potentiometric Auto Titrator: Chloride & ionic content
Halogen Moisture Analyser: Fast loss-on-drying verification
Muffle Furnace: High-temperature ash content analysis
Bead Mill (Dispermat): Laboratory pigment wet milling
Vibroshaker (Red Devil): Paint & pigment dispersion testing
Automatic Muller: Standard pigment tinting strength
Parallel Rotating Bars: Fine milling & homogenizing
Ink Proofer: Inking printability & drawdown kit
Hegman Gauge: Grind fineness & dispersion rating
Rotational Viscometer & Zahn Cup: Viscosity profiling
Gloss Meter & Flow Glass: Surface gloss & levelling
Conductivity Meter: Electrolyte & salt measurement
Mettler Analytical Balance: Micro-precision weighing
