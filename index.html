Key Applications

Colorants Tailored For Diverse Applications

Paper & Pulp

Optimized Basic and Direct Dyes together with pigment dispersions for commercial paper coloring.

Writing Instruments

Custom-grade ballpoint pen inks, marker inks, ribbon inks, and child-safe EN-71 compliant solutions.

Printing Inks & Art Supplies

High-performance violet and blue toners alongside rinsable dyes for consumer arts and crafts.

Textiles, Leather & Wool

Vibrant, high-affinity Acid and Direct Dyes engineered for silk, nylon, wool, and leather treatments.

Wood & Turf Coloring

Robust colorants designed to withstand environmental conditions for MDF, treated wood, and lawn turf coloring.

Digital Ink & Coatings

Advanced organic and inorganic pigment dispersions engineered for conventional and digital inkjet printer applications.
